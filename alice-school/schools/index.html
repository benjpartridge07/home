🏫 Secondary School

Alice is in Year 6, so she starts secondary school in September 2027. Here are the schools she could go to from Newport, and how and when to apply. Checked 12 October 2026.

⏰ Apply by Saturday 31 October 2026

Applications for September 2027 are open now.

Apply online through Telford & Wrekin Council, even for schools in Shropshire. You can list up to 4 schools in order of preference. Applications after 31 October are only looked at once all the on-time ones are done, so popular schools may already be full.

Apply at telford.gov.uk/admissions →

📅 Key dates

  • September–October 2026School open evenings. Most have already happened, but schools will usually show you around if you phone.
  • 31 October 2026: closing dateThe online application must be in by this date.
  • 1 March 2027: National Offer DayThe council emails you which school Alice has been offered.
  • 31 March 2027Deadline to appeal if you don't get the school you want. Appeals are heard April to June.
  • 30 June 2027Deadline to apply for free school travel (bus pass) if Alice qualifies.
  • 6–7 July 2027Year 6 children visit their new school.
  • September 2027Alice starts Year 7.

Catchment school

Newport (Audley Avenue, TF10) · mixed, ages 11–16 · Learning Community Trust

The safe choice. Always include it on the form, even if it isn't first. Website

💡 Good to know

  • Putting a school second doesn't hurt your chances there. Each school looks at every application by its own rules, without seeing your ranking. If Alice qualifies for more than one, she's offered the highest one on your list. So put the school you like most first.
  • Use all 4 choices and always include Burton Borough (the catchment school). Listing only one school gives no extra priority.
  • If no listed school has room, the council offers a place at the catchment school or the nearest school with space.
  • Free bus pass: Alice may get one if she goes to her nearest or catchment school and it's over 3 miles away. Lower-income families can also get help for one of the 3 nearest suitable schools between 2 and 6 miles away. Apply at travelassistance.telford.gov.uk by 30 June 2027.
  • Free school meals: from September 2026, children in households on Universal Credit get free school meals. It's worth checking it carries on into Year 7.
  • Questions: Telford & Wrekin School Admissions, admissions@telford.gov.uk.
