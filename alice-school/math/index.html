Dividing Large Numbers

Done

Dividing numbers with up to 4 digits by 1- and 2-digit numbers, the Year 6 way: short division (bus stop), writing out the multiples, division using factors, long division, and remainders as r, fractions, decimals or rounded in real-life problems.
