Leader & Support

Which Ork characters can join which squads, using the Warboss/Bigboss/Weirdboy/Bannernob/Painboy line-up from the Armageddon box — plus what a "Leader" and a "Bodyguard unit" actually are.

Warhammer 40,000 · 11th Edition · Orks

Leader & Support

Two names for the two halves of the same relationship: the Leader is the CHARACTER model that attaches, the Bodyguard unit is the squad it joins. Every eligible pairing is spelled out on the datasheets themselves — there's no army-wide rule to memorise, just two lists that mirror each other.

01

Leader vs Bodyguard unit

Leader is the ability printed on a CHARACTER's own datasheet: "This model can be attached to the following units." It lists every squad that character is allowed to join.

Bodyguard unit is the squad's side of the same relationship — its datasheet carries a matching "LED BY" section naming every character allowed to attach to it. The two lists always agree with each other; they're just printed from opposite ends.

Once attached, the Leader and its Bodyguard unit act as one combined unit for the rest of the game — they move, shoot, charge and fight together, and are treated as a single unit for coherency. Each model still keeps its own profile though (its own wounds, weapons and attacks) rather than being merged into one averaged statline.

A separate ability: Support

Some characters (Bannernob, Painboy) have a Support ability instead of Leader — mechanically it works the same way (attach to a listed unit), but it's a different slot. A unit can have both a Leader and a Support character attached to it at the same time — e.g. a Boyz squad could carry a Warboss (Leader) and a Painboy (Support) together.

03

Gretchin

Gretchin's own "LED BY" list names only two characters — Da Red Gobbo and Zodgrod Wortsnagga, both Epic Heroes. Neither is in the Armageddon box, so with what's actually in your collection, the Gretchin squad will always sit on the table unled.

That's specific to which characters exist for Gretchin though, not a general rule about them — if you ever pick up one of those two models, they could attach. And either way, Gretchin can never merge with a Boyz squad or any other rank-and-file unit: only a single CHARACTER model can attach to a unit, ordinary squads never combine with each other.
