Weapon Abilities

Every Weapon Ability — the keywords printed under an individual weapon's profile on a datasheet, like Rapid Fire or Cleave — listed A–Z with what it does. These are different from Datasheet Abilities (whole-unit rules like Deep Strike or Stealth), which live on the datasheet itself rather than under a specific weapon.

Warhammer 40,000 · 11th Edition

Weapon Abilities, A–Z

Any keyword you see written under a weapon's stat line (Rapid Fire 1, Devastating Wounds, Cleave 1, and so on) is a Weapon Ability — it only affects attacks made with that specific weapon. Values shown as “X” vary per weapon — check the datasheet for the actual number.

⚠️ Double-check before relying on these at the table

11th edition reshuffled several weapon abilities from 10th (Devastating Wounds, Hazardous, Heavy, Indirect Fire and Precision all work differently now), and added Cleave as brand new. The Pistol keyword's exact current name is genuinely unsettled across sources at time of writing — some call it Point-Blank, others say it's just Pistol again and functionally identical to Close-Quarters shooting. Confirm exact wording and values on Wahapedia, which stays current as rules get FAQ'd/errata'd.

Anti-X Y+

Against models with keyword X, this weapon scores a Critical Wound on an unmodified wound roll of Y+ (e.g. Anti-Vehicle 3+ critically wounds Vehicles on a 3, 4, 5 or 6).

Assault

This weapon can still be fired even if the bearer's unit Advanced this turn.

Blast

Add 1 to this weapon's Attacks characteristic for every full 5 models in the target unit — rewards firing into big blobs.

Cleave XNew in 11th

The melee equivalent of Blast — adds bonus Attacks against a target unit based on its model count, but on a melee weapon rather than a ranged one.

Exact wording/value mechanics not yet double-checked against a primary source — verify on Wahapedia before relying on it.

Conversion X+

Scores a Critical Hit on an unmodified hit roll of X+ when the target is beyond half this weapon's range — rewards long-range precision shooting.

Devastating WoundsChanged

A Critical Wound scored by this weapon ignores the target's saving throw entirely, including any invulnerable save.

In 10th edition this instead converted the hit into mortal wounds.

Extra Attacks

Can't be chosen as a bearer's only weapon. When resolving attacks, add this weapon's Attacks to those of another weapon the model is also attacking with that phase.

HazardousChanged

After the weapon is used, roll a D6 for each model that used it; on a 1–2, that model's unit suffers 1 mortal wound (3 for VEHICLE units).

In 10th edition this only failed on a 1, and the mortal-wound count scaled with unit size rather than being a flat number.

HeavyChanged

Get +1 to hit with this weapon if the bearer's unit is not Engaged and moved 3" or less this turn.

In 10th edition this required the unit to Remain Stationary entirely; 11th allows a short move and still grants the bonus.

Ignores Cover

The target unit does not get the Benefit of Cover against attacks made with this weapon.

Indirect FireChanged

This weapon can target units the bearer can't draw line of sight to, at reduced accuracy (or full accuracy if an allied unit can see the target).

Reworked significantly for 11th edition — confirm the current penalty/observer mechanics on Wahapedia rather than assuming 10th-edition rules still apply.

Lance

Get +1 to wound with this weapon if the bearer's unit made a Charge move this turn.

Lethal Hits

An unmodified hit roll of 6 (a Critical Hit) with this weapon automatically wounds the target — no wound roll needed for that hit.

Melta X

Add X to this weapon's Damage characteristic when the target is within half range.

One Shot

This weapon can only be selected to shoot with once per battle.

PistolNaming unsettled

The bearer's unit can still fire this weapon even while Engaged with the enemy (within Engagement Range) — other, non-Pistol weapons can't.

10th edition's [PISTOL] keyword; some 11th-edition sources call the current version [POINT-BLANK], others keep [PISTOL] and treat it as functionally identical to Close-Quarters shooting. Check the exact current name on Wahapedia.

PrecisionChanged

Attacks made with this weapon can be allocated to CHARACTER models within the target unit, not just the closest eligible model — lets you snipe out an attached character.

Psychic

Marks the weapon as a psychic attack, relevant to any rule that specifically buffs, blocks, or reacts to Psychic Attacks (e.g. anti-psyker abilities).

Some sources describe an additional 11th-edition effect around ignoring hit-roll modifiers — unconfirmed, verify on Wahapedia.

Rapid Fire X

Add X to this weapon's Attacks characteristic when the target is within half range.

Sustained Hits X

A Critical Hit with this weapon scores X additional hits on the target, on top of the original hit.

Torrent

This weapon automatically hits its target — no hit roll is made.

Twin-linked

Re-roll wound rolls made for this weapon.
