eBay tips

  • Check sold prices first (last link above). That's what things are really worth, not what sellers are asking.
  • "NoS" or "new on sprue" means unbuilt and unpainted, which is the best value.
  • Big mixed lots of built or painted models are usually the cheapest per model. Strip the paint with isopropyl alcohol.
  • Avoid listings that say "3D printed", "recast" or "proxy", or have stock photos and prices that look too good to be true.
  • Stick to sellers with 98%+ feedback, and pay through eBay so you're covered by the Money Back Guarantee.

🎨 Other offers

  • Warhammer stores, paint pick 'n' mix: buy any 10 Warhammer Colour paints and the most expensive one is free.
  • Warhammer stores, from Sat 10 Oct: free build or paint sessions, plus free collectable coins on 3 Oct and 24 Oct while stocks last.
  • Element Games: free UK delivery on orders over £80.
  • Black Friday (late November): Wayland, Element and Amazon usually add extra discounts on top. It's a good time for big boxes like Combat Patrols.
