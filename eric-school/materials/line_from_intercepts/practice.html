Equation from Intercepts

You're told where a straight line crosses the axes. Write its equation.

How to do it

  1. c is where the line crosses the y-axis. Crosses at (0, 6)? Then c = 6.
  2. Gradient m = change in y ÷ change in x. From (0, 6) to (3, 0): y goes down 6 while x goes along 3, so m = −6 ÷ 3 = −2.
  3. Put them together: y = −2x + 6.
  4. Check: put x = 3 in. Do you get y = 0?

Type fractions with a slash, e.g. y = -2/3x + 4. Press Enter to check.
