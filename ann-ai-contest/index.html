1

Sales · Customer retention

Renewal & Account Continuity Engine

⭐ Top pick, Ann’s own idea

Today: Renewal dates for yearly maintenance, licence and support contracts live in each salesperson’s memory or personal notes. When someone is busy or leaves, renewals can be missed and the customer relationship is at risk.

With Generative AI: AI reads contracts, invoices and CRM records, builds one shared renewal list, and reminds the account owner 90, 60 and 30 days before each contract ends. It also drafts the renewal proposal from the original terms. When someone leaves, AI writes a one-page handover brief for each of their accounts, so nothing depends on one person.

01
Extract
AI pulls end dates, values and contacts out of contract PDFs and spreadsheets
02
Remind
Staged reminders to the owner and their manager in Outlook or Teams
03
Draft
A first-draft renewal proposal based on last year’s terms
04
Hand over
Account briefs created automatically when staff change roles
ImpactProtects recurring revenue; a missed renewal is easy to put a THB value on
ScalabilityEvery NSSOL Group company sells contracts that renew each year
InnovationMoves from relying on memory to a shared, AI-managed process
TechnicalDocument extraction plus Copilot / Power Automate reminders

Measure for the entry: how many renewals are tracked; renewals caught early that would otherwise have been late; hours saved preparing renewal proposals; and how long a handover takes before and after.

2

Pre-sales · Proposals

RFP-to-Proposal Copilot

Strong runner-up

Today: Each customer RFP or TOR (often in Thai, English or Japanese) is read by hand, requirements are copied into spreadsheets, and proposals are rebuilt from old files. A big ERP proposal can take days of senior staff time.

With Generative AI: AI reads the RFP, lists every requirement, matches each one to the ERP module or past project that covers it, flags gaps and risks, and drafts the proposal and a first estimate based on similar past projects. Staff review and refine it instead of starting from a blank page.

01
Read
Turn the RFP into a structured requirements list in any language
02
Match
Map requirements to standard ERP features and past solutions
03
Draft
Proposal sections, a compliance matrix and a draft estimate
04
Learn
Won and lost proposals feed back in to improve the next one
ImpactFaster replies and more bids handled by the same team
ScalabilityEvery group company answers RFPs
InnovationWorks across languages, which suits Thai, Japanese and English customers
TechnicalSearch over past proposals (RAG) plus structured outputs

Measure for the entry: hours to the first draft, before and after, on 2–3 real past RFPs; the share of requirements AI mapped correctly; and how many requirements it caught that people had missed.

ERP delivery · Requirements

Workshop-to-Fit-Gap Assistant

Today: ERP projects start with many requirement workshops. Consultants take notes, write minutes, then spend more days turning them into fit-gap documents and specifications, and details get lost along the way.

With Generative AI: Teams meeting transcripts are turned straight into requirement lists. AI classifies each one as standard fit, configuration or customisation, drafts the fit-gap document and functional specs, and keeps a link from every requirement back to the meeting where it was agreed.

01
Capture
Transcribe workshops (Teams + Copilot)
02
Classify
Fit, configure or customise, by business process
03
Document
Draft fit-gap and specs in the company template
04
Trace
Each requirement links back to its source meeting
ImpactShorter analysis phase, fewer missed requirements and disputes later
ScalabilityFits every ERP and system-integration project, in any group company
InnovationTurns meetings directly into deliverables
TechnicalSpeech-to-text, classification and template generation

Measure for the entry: days from the last workshop to an approved fit-gap document; how many requirements the customer changed later because they were missed.

Support · After go-live

Multilingual Support Desk Copilot

Today: After go-live, support tickets and emails arrive in mixed languages. The same questions are answered again and again, and fixes known only to one engineer are hard to find.

With Generative AI: AI sorts each ticket by module and urgency, searches past tickets and manuals for the likely fix, and drafts a reply in the customer’s language for the engineer to check. Every solved ticket becomes a new knowledge-base article automatically.

01
Triage
Sort by module, priority and customer
02
Suggest
Likely fixes from past tickets and manuals
03
Reply
Draft answers in Thai, English or Japanese
04
Learn
Solved tickets become knowledge articles
ImpactFaster response and resolution, so customers are happier at renewal time
ScalabilityAny group company with a support desk
InnovationA knowledge base that keeps itself up to date
TechnicalRAG over ticket history, with a person approving each reply

Measure for the entry: average first-response time and resolution time; the share of AI-drafted replies sent with little editing; and the number of knowledge articles created.

Project management · PMO

Project Health Early-Warning System

Today: Project problems such as slipping tasks, growing issue lists and scope creep often show up in status reports and emails weeks before anyone escalates them. By then, the cost of a late or over-budget project is already high.

With Generative AI: Each week, AI reads status reports, issue logs, timesheets and project emails, summarises every project in a few lines for management, and flags risk signals early, with a suggested action.

02
Summarise

03
Flag
Spot risk patterns such as scope creep and repeated delays
04
Act
Suggested next steps and escalation
ImpactFewer late or loss-making projects
ScalabilityWorks for any project portfolio, so it suits group-wide use
InnovationCatches problems early instead of reporting them afterwards
TechnicalSummarisation plus risk-signal detection

Measure for the entry: time management spends preparing status reviews; how many weeks earlier risks were flagged on past projects (test it on old project data).

ERP delivery · Data migration

AI Data Migration & Cleansing Assistant

New

Today: Moving a customer’s old data (customers, suppliers, items, bills of materials) into a new ERP is slow, manual spreadsheet work. Duplicates, missing fields and mixed Thai and English spellings cause go-live delays.

With Generative AI: AI profiles the old data, finds duplicates and errors (for example, the same supplier spelled three ways in Thai and English), suggests how each old field maps to the new ERP, and produces a cleansing report for the customer to approve.

01
Profile
Scan old data for gaps, duplicates and odd values
02
Map
Suggest old-to-new field mappings
03
Cleanse
Propose fixes and merged records for approval
04
Validate
Check the loaded data against the source
ImpactFewer migration rounds and fewer go-live delays
ScalabilityEvery ERP project has a data migration
InnovationUnderstands Thai and English names together
TechnicalData profiling plus AI matching, with people approving

Measure for the entry: hours per migration round, before and after; duplicates found; and the number of test loads needed before the data is clean.

Quality · Testing

ERP Test Script & UAT Generator

New

Today: Test scripts for system testing and user acceptance testing (UAT) are written by hand from specifications. It takes a long time, coverage depends on who writes them, and regression tests are often skipped when there is a rush.

With Generative AI: AI reads the fit-gap document and specifications and writes test scenarios and step-by-step UAT scripts in the customer’s language, including edge cases. When a change is made, it lists which tests need to run again.

02
Generate
Write test scenarios and UAT scripts
03
Prioritise
Pick the regression tests that matter for each change
04
Report
Summarise results and defects for the customer
ImpactFaster, more complete testing and fewer bugs after go-live
ScalabilityWorks for any software project in the group
InnovationTesting linked directly to requirements
TechnicalPairs well with Idea 3 (fit-gap) as one flow

Measure for the entry: hours to prepare UAT scripts, before and after; the number of test cases; and defects found after go-live compared with similar past projects.

Making it a winning entry

Show real numbers

Judges want “tangible results”. Run a small pilot and report before-and-after hours, days or THB, even from a sample of 10–20 cases.

Explain how it spreads

Scalability is scored, so show how another NSSOL Group company could reuse it with its own data: same steps, same tools.

Include a simple diagram

For Technical Excellence, show where the data comes from, which approved AI tool does what, where a person checks the output, and how customer data stays secure.

Tell it as a story

Problem, then a real example, then the AI process, then results, then what’s next. The finalists present on 17 Feb, so a short live demo helps.
